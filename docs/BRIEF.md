# Qué cenamos hoy — Project Brief

> A personal PWA that answers the daily question "¿Qué cenamos hoy?" with AI-powered dinner suggestions based on what I love and what I've eaten recently.

- **URL:** `quecenamoshoy.helloimtom.dev`
- **Users:** one (me). Never public, never multi-user.
- **Devices:** Mac (Chrome) and Google Pixel (installed PWA).
- **Budget:** €0, except Gemini usage on my existing paid API key.

---

## 1. Problem

Nearly every evening, the question is "what do we eat tonight?", and nobody knows. I keep a list of dishes I like, but:

- I keep cooking the same things.
- I never save recipes anywhere.
- The list doesn't help me decide. It just sits there.

## 2. Goal

Open the app, answer a few quick questions, and get 3 dinner ideas that:

- fit what I feel like tonight (hot/cold, leftovers, who is eating),
- are safe and liked by everyone at the table: nobody's allergies, nobody's dislikes (3.8),
- avoid what I ate in the last few days,
- lean on dishes and flavours I keep coming back to, while proposing new ones.
- If you noticed from frequency that one dish is a whole time favorite, and that it's been long enough I didn't prepare it, you may also suggest it. The idea is an app that learns from my habits and tastes.
- Based on my tastes it should also try to make sure my diet is varied. Like, don't offer beef meat based meals 3 days in a row.

My favourites list stays at the core, but each dish becomes a proper card with an image and a recipe.

## 3. Features (MVP)

### 3.1 Auth
- Supabase Auth, email + password (or magic link), one account.
- **Sign-ups disabled** in the Supabase dashboard after creating my account.
- Every screen requires a session.

### 3.2 "Qué cenamos hoy" wizard
A step-by-step flow, one question per screen, big tap targets, thumb-friendly on the Pixel:

| Step | Question | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ¿Dónde estás hoy? | En casa · De viaje |
| 2 | ¿Frío o caliente? | Caliente · Frío · Me da igual |
| 3 | ¿Necesitas sobras para comer mañana? | Sí · No |
| 4 | ¿Quién come hoy? | Profile chips (multi-select, default: me + my boyfriend) + **Invitados** stepper (default 0) |
| 5 | Results | 3 suggestions |

**Where am I today** decides which kitchen the suggestions are cooked in (see 3.6):
- **En casa**: my kitchen inventory, exactly as saved.
- **De viaje**: a minimal kitchen (hob, one pan, one pot, a knife), editable on the spot for that
  trip ("there's an oven here"). Leftovers default to **No** when travelling.

**Who eats today** sets the number of portions (selected profiles + guests) and pulls in
everyone's favourites and restrictions (3.8). A guest can get one-off restrictions on the spot
("one guest is vegetarian", "nut allergy"), used for that meal only.

Location and diners are remembered for the day, so the roulette (3.7) does not ask them again.

Each suggestion card shows the name, a short description, an image, prep time, and a tag: **Favourite** or **New idea**.

From a suggestion I can:
- **"Cenamos esto"**: logs it as tonight's meal.
- **See recipe**: full ingredients and steps, scaled to the number of people (+1 portion per person when leftovers are needed).
- **Add to favourites**: for new AI ideas I want to keep.
- **Regenerate**: get 3 other ideas with the same answers.

### 3.3 Favourites
- Favourites belong to **people** (3.8): mine, my boyfriend's, or both. One dish, one card, with
  the faces of whoever loves it. Filter: "Mis favoritos" · "De [name]" · "De los dos".
- List or grid of the favourite dishes as illustrated cards.
- Add, edit and delete a dish by hand.
- Each dish can have a recipe. If it has none, **"Generate recipe with AI"** fills it in, and I can edit it.
- Import my existing list once (paste text, one dish per line). The AI enriches each dish with temp, tags and a description.

### 3.4 History
- Chronological list of what we ate, when, and **who was at the table**.
- Log a meal manually (for nights I didn't use the wizard).
- Optional 1–5 rating and note per meal ("too spicy", "make again").

### 3.5 Images
- Every dish has an image stored in Supabase Storage.
- Source: my own photo (upload from camera) **or** generated by Gemini.

### 3.6 My kitchen (toolbox)
A page that knows every appliance and tool I own, so the app only ever proposes what I can
actually cook. Example: I have no air fryer, so no suggestion, roulette pick or generated recipe
ever needs one.

- **Scan**: take one or more photos of the kitchen (worktop, cupboards, drawers). Gemini lists
  the appliances and tools it recognises; I confirm, untick mistakes, and add what it missed.
- **Edit by hand**: a checklist grouped by category: appliances (oven, microwave, air fryer,
  slow cooker, pressure cooker, blender, food processor, stand mixer…), cookware (pans, pots,
  wok, roasting tray, casserole…), tools (grater, mandoline, thermometer, scales…).
- **Every recipe declares its equipment** (`equipment: ["oven", "frying pan"]`). A recipe or dish
  whose equipment I don't have is never suggested, never picked by the roulette, and a recipe
  generated for an existing dish is written with what I have (e.g. oven instead of air fryer).
- The travelling kitchen (3.2) is a second, smaller inventory.

### 3.7 Roulette (random pick)
For nights when I don't want to answer questions, just spin.

1. **First, always**: "¿Alguna preferencia hoy?" A free text field plus quick chips
   ("algo cremoso", "más pasta", "ligero", "nada de carne"). Skippable with "Sorpréndeme".
   Example: I had carbonara last night and still feel like something creamy, or more pasta.
2. The preference becomes a filter and a weight, not a hard rule: dishes that match it get more
   slots on the wheel, the rest stay possible.
3. **Spin**: an animated wheel of 6–8 candidates from my favourites (plus AI ideas I've saved),
   already filtered by the same rules as the suggestions: avoid window, variety, equipment (3.6),
   where I am today and who is eating (3.2, 3.8).
4. Result card, with the same actions as a suggestion: "Cenamos esto", see recipe, spin again.
   **Spin again keeps the preference**; changing it means going back to step 1.

### 3.8 People, favourites and dietary needs
Still **one account** (mine), but the app knows the people I cook for.

- **Profiles**: me (always there), my boyfriend, and anyone I add (family, regular friends).
  A name and an optional photo or initial.
- **Per person**:
  - **Favourites** (3.3), and the frequency signals of 2 (all-time favourites not cooked for a while).
  - **Allergies and intolerances** (e.g. nuts, shellfish, lactose): a **hard** rule. A dish or
    recipe with that ingredient is never suggested, never on the roulette, and a generated
    recipe never contains it, whenever that person eats.
  - **Dislikes** (e.g. coriander, olives, liver): a **soft** rule. Avoided when that person eats;
    a recipe may say "sin cilantro" instead of dropping the dish.
  - **Diet** if any (vegetarian, no pork…): treated like an allergy.
- **Eating together**: the restrictions of everyone at the table add up; the favourites of
  everyone count, and a dish loved by several of us ranks higher.
- **Safety net for allergies**: the AI is not trusted alone. The app also checks every
  suggested recipe's ingredients against the diners' allergens (a fixed allergen catalogue with
  synonyms: "nata" = lactose, "piñones" = nuts) and shows a clear warning instead of the
  "Cenamos esto" button when something matches.

### 3.9 Settings — eating goals and preferences
A settings page with checkboxes, so the suggestions push me where I want to go, not only
where my habits are.

- **Eating goals** (per person, checkbox + a target I can adjust):
  - Limit meat, e.g. "carne roja como máximo **2×** por semana"
  - Try vegan dishes, e.g. "al menos **1** plato vegano por semana"
  - More fish, more vegetables, more legumes
  - Less fried food, lighter dinners on weekdays
- Goals are **soft nudges**, never hard rules (allergies and diets in 3.8 are the hard ones):
  - The suggestions and the roulette count what was eaten in the last 7 days and weight the
    options toward the goals that are behind ("Llevas 3 días con carne roja → hoy pescado").
  - A goal never blocks a dish; it just stops being favoured once it is met.
  - When eating together, the goals of everyone at the table count.
- **Progress**: a small weekly summary on the history page ("Esta semana: 1/2 carne roja,
  0/1 vegano").
- **Other settings**: language (es / en / fr), theme (light / dark / system), reduced motion,
  the avoid window (default 7 days), default diners for the wizard, my profile and the people
  I cook for (3.8), my kitchen (3.6), sign out.

## 4. AI behaviour

Gemini is called **only** from a Supabase Edge Function, never from the browser.

### 4.1 Context sent with each suggestion request
- Wizard answers: `location`, `diners` (profile ids), `guests`, `temp`, `leftovers`.
- For each diner: favourites, allergies, dislikes, diet (3.8), eating goals (3.9). Plus any one-off guest restrictions.
- Goal counters for the last 7 days (e.g. red meat 3, vegan 0).
- Kitchen equipment available for that location (3.6).
- Favourites: name, temp, tags (compact).
- Meals from the **last 14 days**: name + date.
- Highly rated past meals and recurring tags, as "taste signals".
- Today's date (for seasonality).

### 4.2 Rules given to the model
- Return exactly 3 suggestions.
- Never suggest a dish eaten in the **avoid window** (setting, default 7 days).
- Mix: ideally 1–2 favourites + 1–2 new ideas inspired by my tastes.
- If leftovers are needed, prefer dishes that keep and reheat well.
- Respect hot/cold unless "Me da igual".
- **Never** suggest a dish that needs equipment I don't have in today's kitchen.
- **Never** suggest a dish containing an allergen or breaking the diet of anyone at the table; avoid their dislikes.
- Prefer dishes several diners have as favourites.
- Nudge toward eating goals that are behind; never force them (3.9).
- Respond in the app's current language (es, en or fr).

### 4.3 Structured output
Use `responseSchema` (JSON mode), never free text:

```json
{
  "suggestions": [
    {
      "dishId": "uuid | null",
      "name": "string",
      "description": "string",
      "temp": "hot | cold",
      "prepMinutes": 30,
      "goodForLeftovers": true,
      "tags": ["pasta", "horno"],
      "equipment": ["oven", "pot"],
      "isFavourite": true,
      "reason": "string"
    }
  ]
}
```

`dishId` is set when the suggestion is an existing favourite. `reason` is one short line ("No has comido pasta esta semana").

Recipes are generated on demand (when I open one with no recipe), with their own schema:

```json
{
  "servings": 3,
  "prepMinutes": 30,
  "equipment": ["string"],
  "ingredients": [{ "item": "string", "quantity": "string" }],
  "steps": ["string"]
}
```

### 4.4 Models
- Text: `gemini-2.5-flash`.
- Images: Gemini image model, generated once per dish and stored, never regenerated on each view.
- SDK: **`@google/genai`** (not the deprecated `@google/generative-ai`).
- API key: a **new key** in its own Google AI Studio project `quecenamoshoy`, linked to the same paid billing account as tom-dev, stored as a Supabase secret (`GEMINI_API_KEY`).

## 6. Data model

All tables use row-level security (RLS): rows are visible only where `user_id = auth.uid()`.

```sql
create type dish_temp as enum ('hot', 'cold');
create type dish_source as enum ('mine', 'ai');

create table dishes (
  id            uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id       uuid not null references auth.users on delete cascade,
  name          text not null,
  description   text,
  temp          dish_temp,
  tags          text[] not null default '{}',
  is_favourite  boolean not null default false,   -- superseded by dish_favourites (below)
  source        dish_source not null default 'mine',
  image_path    text,              -- Supabase Storage path
  created_at    timestamptz not null default now()
);

create table recipes (
  id             uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  dish_id        uuid not null unique references dishes on delete cascade,
  servings       int not null,
  prep_minutes   int,
  good_for_leftovers boolean not null default false,
  ingredients    jsonb not null,   -- [{ item, quantity }]
  steps          jsonb not null,   -- [string]
  updated_at     timestamptz not null default now()
);

create table meals (
  id             uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id        uuid not null references auth.users on delete cascade,
  dish_id        uuid not null references dishes on delete restrict,
  eaten_on       date not null default current_date,
  people         int not null default 2,
  with_leftovers boolean not null default false,
  rating         smallint check (rating between 1 and 5),
  note           text,
  created_at     timestamptz not null default now()
);

create index on meals (user_id, eaten_on desc);

create type kitchen_location as enum ('home', 'travel');

-- 3.6 — one row per piece of equipment, per kitchen
create table kitchen_items (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id    uuid not null references auth.users on delete cascade,
  location   kitchen_location not null default 'home',
  key        text not null,          -- stable id from a fixed catalogue: 'oven', 'air_fryer'…
  category   text not null,          -- 'appliance' | 'cookware' | 'tool'
  source     dish_source not null default 'mine',   -- 'ai' when found by a scan
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  unique (user_id, location, key)
);

alter table dishes  add column equipment text[] not null default '{}';  -- filled by import-list / AI enrichment, so a favourite with no recipe can still be filtered
alter table recipes add column equipment text[] not null default '{}';
alter table meals   add column location kitchen_location not null default 'home';
```

-- 3.9 — what a dish IS, so goals can be counted. Set by hand or by AI enrichment.
create type dish_protein as enum ('red_meat', 'pork', 'poultry', 'fish', 'seafood', 'eggs', 'vegetarian', 'vegan');
alter table dishes add column protein dish_protein;
alter table dishes add column traits text[] not null default '{}';   -- 'fried', 'light', 'legumes', 'vegetables'… (catalogue)

create table person_goals (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  person_id  uuid not null references people on delete cascade,
  key        text not null,              -- catalogue: 'limit_red_meat', 'try_vegan', 'more_fish'…
  per_week   int not null check (per_week between 0 and 7),   -- max for 'limit_*', min for the others
  unique (person_id, key)
);

create table settings (                  -- one row per account
  user_id          uuid primary key references auth.users on delete cascade,
  avoid_days       int not null default 7 check (avoid_days between 0 and 30),
  default_diners   uuid[] not null default '{}'
);
-- language, theme and reduced motion are device preferences: localStorage, not the database

-- 3.8 — the people I cook for (still one account: user_id = me)
create table people (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id    uuid not null references auth.users on delete cascade,
  name       text not null,
  is_me      boolean not null default false,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now()
);

create table dish_favourites (           -- who loves which dish
  person_id  uuid not null references people on delete cascade,
  dish_id    uuid not null references dishes on delete cascade,
  primary key (person_id, dish_id)
);

create type restriction_kind as enum ('allergy', 'dislike', 'diet');
create table person_restrictions (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  person_id  uuid not null references people on delete cascade,
  kind       restriction_kind not null,
  key        text not null,              -- from a fixed catalogue: 'nuts', 'lactose', 'coriander', 'vegetarian'…
  unique (person_id, kind, key)
);

create table meal_diners (               -- who ate this meal
  meal_id    uuid not null references meals on delete cascade,
  person_id  uuid not null references people on delete cascade,
  primary key (meal_id, person_id)
);
alter table meals add column guests int not null default 0;
-- meals.people stays as the portion count (diners + guests)

Restriction keys come from fixed catalogues (`constants/allergens.ts`, `constants/diets.ts`),
translated in `locales/`, with ingredient synonyms for the allergen safety net (3.8).

Equipment keys come from one fixed catalogue in the app (`constants/equipment.ts`), translated
in `locales/`. The AI must answer with those keys only, so "horno" and "oven" are the same item.

Choosing a **new AI idea** with "Cenamos esto" first creates a `dishes` row (`source = 'ai'`), then the `meals` row and its `meal_diners`. Every meal points to a real dish, and the dish can be added to favourites later.

**Storage:** one private bucket `dish-images`, path `{user_id}/{dish_id}.webp`.

## 7. Edge Functions

All require a valid Supabase JWT (default behaviour).

| Function | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `suggest` | `{ location, diners: string[], guests: number, guestRestrictions?: string[], temp: 'hot' \| 'cold' \| 'any', leftovers: boolean, exclude?: string[] }` | `{ suggestions: [...] }` (see 4.3) |
| `recipe` | `{ dishId, diners, guests, leftovers, location }` (portions, allergies, dislikes and equipment from those) | Recipe JSON, saved to `recipes` |
| `dish-image` | `{ dishId }` | Generates an image, uploads it to Storage, sets `image_path` |
| `import-list` | `{ text }` | Creates `dishes` rows (favourites), enriched with temp, tags and description |
| `scan-kitchen` | `{ imagePaths: string[] }` (photos uploaded to Storage first) | `{ items: [{ key, category, confidence }] }`, keys from the catalogue only. Nothing is saved until I confirm. |
| `roulette` | `{ location, diners, guests, preference?: string }` | `{ candidates: [{ dishId, weight, reason }] }` (6–8). The wheel spins in the app; the function only filters and weights. |

The `exclude` field holds the names already shown, so **Regenerate** never repeats them.

## 9. Code conventions

- One exported component per file.
- Imports via `@/` alias only, no `../../`.
- Tailwind v4, CSS-only config, tokens in `@theme` inside `globals.css`. No hardcoded colours, px or font names in components, and no inline `style` for colours, fonts or borders.
- Semantic text classes with descriptive names (`.display`, `.heading`, `.body-text`, `.label`), never Tailwind-like names.
- No native `<select>`, checkbox or date inputs: custom primitives in `components/ui/`.
- No third-party component libraries (no shadcn, radix or headless-ui).
- No `console.*`: use `logger.*` from `utils/logger.ts`.
- Remove dead CSS, unused imports and orphaned files in the same change.
- Strings in `locales/`.
